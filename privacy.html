Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our YouTube automation application, we may collect information regarding your YouTube channel in order to upload videos automatically via the YouTube Data API. We do not store your credentials on any remote server; everything runs locally on your machine.

2. How We Use Your Information

The application only uses your channel data and Google account access specifically to:

  • Upload generated video shorts to your YouTube channel.
  • Add titles, descriptions, and tags to your uploaded videos.

3. Third-Party Access

We do not share, sell, or distribute your data to any third party. Our application strictly interacts with the YouTube Data API v3.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the repository owner on GitHub.
