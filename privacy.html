Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Brain Shock application, we may collect information regarding your social media channels to automatically upload videos via the YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API. We do not store your credentials on any remote server; everything runs locally on your machine.

2. How We Use Your Information

The application only accesses your channel data and social media access to specifically:

  • Upload generated videos to your YouTube and TikTok channels.
  • Add titles, descriptions, and tags to your uploaded videos.

3. Third-Party Access

We do not share, sell, or distribute your data to any third party. Our application strictly interacts with official API infrastructures.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us through our official channels.
